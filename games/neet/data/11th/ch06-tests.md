**Paper 2 (Q1–Q30): The Ground Tissue System and The Vascular Tissue System**
1. Ground tissue — all tissue except epidermis/vascular bundles
2. Simple tissues — parenchyma, collenchyma, sclerenchyma
3. Ground tissue locations — cortex, pericycle, pith, medullary rays
4. Mesophyll — leaf-specific ground tissue
5. Vascular system — xylem and phloem, complex tissues
6. Vascular bundles — xylem + phloem together
7. Open vascular bundles — cambium present, dicots, secondary growth
8. Closed vascular bundles — cambium absent, monocots, no secondary growth
9. Radial arrangement — alternating xylem/phloem, roots
10. Conjoint arrangement — xylem/phloem same radius, stems/leaves
11. Phloem position in conjoint bundles (outer side of xylem)
12. Ground tissue forming main bulk of the plant
13. Vascular bundle function — translocation of water, minerals, food
