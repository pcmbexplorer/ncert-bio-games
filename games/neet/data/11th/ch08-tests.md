## Concept Index — Cell: The Unit of Life

**Paper 1 (Q1–Q30): What is a Cell?, Cell Theory and An Overview of the Cell**
1. Cell — fundamental structural and functional unit of all living organisms

10. Final cell theory — two tenets
11. Onion peel cell — cell wall outer boundary, cell membrane within
12. Human cheek cell — outer membrane as delimiting structure

14. Eukaryotic vs prokaryotic — based on membrane-bound nucleus
15. Cytoplasm — semi-fluid matrix, main arena of cellular activities
16. Organelles found only in eukaryotic cells
17. Ribosomes — non-membrane bound, found in all cells, also in chloroplasts/mitochondria/rough ER
18. Centrosome — non-membrane bound, animal cells, aids cell division
19. Mycoplasmas — smallest cells, about 0.3 µm
